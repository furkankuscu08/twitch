# Review of wave2/inventory.md

Reviewer: independent audit of the inventory and clustering step. I read brief.md, prompts.md, the five sources and inventory.md in full. I did not read synthesis.md or process.md. I edited no other file.

## Verdict

The inventory is accurate on the mechanical parts and fit to proceed to filtering once a few labels are fixed. A script check found all 101 wide-list ideas (L1 20, L2 22, L3 20, G 22, X 17) present exactly once, with correct IDs and names. All 25 full-card stars are on the right ideas, using wide-list numbering. G's Continuity Crew is correctly G-6, not G-5. Per-cluster source counts, the union crowd-map counts and the convergence table are arithmetically right. The problems are interpretive. The "independent convergence" signal is overstated. Crowd-map labels for K01 and K34 are not supported by the five maps. A few clusters are over-broad (K01, K18, K22). Several parked items and API/test dependencies that full cards rely on are missing from sections 5 and 6. Nothing found looks deliberately unfair, but a few places drop a card's twist. None of these block filtering; fix the high and medium items first.
