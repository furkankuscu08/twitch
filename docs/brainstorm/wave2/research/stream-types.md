# Twitch stream types: what gets watched and what small streamers stream

Research date: 2026-10-26. Most data covers Q1-Q3 2026. Figures are from the sources listed at the end; anything marked "inference" is mine, and anything marked "snippet" comes from a search result summary that I could not open.

## Summary

- **Just Chatting is the largest category by a wide margin.** It is #1 by average viewers (303,611, 14.19% of Twitch viewing) on TwitchTracker, October 2026, and #1 by 30-day hours watched (201.3M) on SullyGnome. Twitch hours in Just Chatting were 668.2M in Q1 2026 and 670.5M in Q2 2026, so they are flat.
- **The top 15 is mostly games.** IRL (#11) is the only non-game category in the top 15, apart from Slots (#12), a slot-machine category.
- **IRL is the fastest-growing non-game category in the sources.** Twitch reported IRL watch time up 186% in 2025. Streams Charts shows it up about 17% in July 2026 and down 12% in August. Music, Art and Talk Shows sit at ranks 40 to 71 with a few thousand viewers each.
- **Twitch is shrinking year on year, and Kick is growing faster.** Twitch hours fell 8.3% in Q2 2026, against Kick's 36.7% rise.
- **Formats with sourced growth are indie games (+51%), Drops (+46%) and horror (+6%).** Esports and creator marathons drive short spikes. Co-streaming, speedrunning and cooking have no usable data in these sources.
- **Small streamers:** I found no public dataset that splits categories by channel size. Viewers per channel are highest in Sports (85), IRL (76) and Just Chatting (66), and lowest in Music (14) and Art (17). These ratios are a proxy for competition, not a measure of it.

## 4. Small streamers (under about 50 average viewers)

No source I could reach splits category data by channel size. The proxies below are from TwitchTracker category pages and Streamlabs.

| Category | Avg viewers | Avg channels | Viewers per channel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sports | 11,930 | 140 | 85.2 |
| IRL | 37,675 | 499 | 75.5 |
| Just Chatting | 303,611 | 4,619 | 65.7 |
| Art | 9,697 | 572 | 17.0 |
| Music | 6,286 | 458 | 13.7 |

- The platform-wide average was 22.6 concurrent viewers per channel in Q2 2026 (Streamlabs/Stream Hatchet). That figure is an average, so large channels inflate it.
- Snippet only, not verified: a StreamHatchet breakdown reports about 61% of channels averaging under 10 viewers, about a third at 11 to 100, and about 6% above 100. The article was not reachable and its date is unknown.
- Inference: a high viewers-per-channel ratio with few channels (Sports) suggests a smaller pool of competing streamers. A low ratio (Music, Art) means many channels share a small audience. Both points rest on averages.
- Growth advice: unverified blog advice suggests aiming for categories with roughly 500 to 5,000 viewers and fewer than 200 live streamers (Eklipse). I could not verify it, so treat it as heuristic.

## 5. Trends 2025 to 2026

- **Twitch is shrinking, and the decline is easing.** Q1 2026: 4.55B hours, down 13.7% YoY. Q2 2026: 4.52B hours, down 8.3% YoY, with QoQ -0.7%.
- **Kick is growing fast.** Q1 2026: 1.27B hours, up 65.4% YoY. Q2 2026: 1.34B hours, up 36.7% YoY.
- **Just Chatting is stable at the top.** Twitch hours were 668.2M in Q1 and 670.5M in Q2 2026.
- **IRL grew sharply in 2025, then mixed in 2026.** It was up 186% in 2025 (Twitch year-end), passed Open World in February 2026, rose about 17% in July, and fell 12% in August.
- **Twitch's own 2025 figure conflicts with the trackers.** Creator Handbook reports Twitch saying "close to 900 million hours of watch time" in 2025. Third-party totals are about 19B. I did not use the Twitch figure.
- **Vertical video:** Twitch announced dual-format streaming in June 2025. No adoption data.
- **Co-streaming:** no sourced data found.

## Data limits

- Streams Charts category pages and the Streams Charts Q2 report returned 403. Their numbers appear only as search snippets.
- The Talk Shows & Podcasts, Software and Game Development, and Food & Drink categories are missing or unverified beyond the rank list.
- Statista's Just Chatting series is masked behind a paywall.
- The Streamlabs Q1 2026 share percentages do not reconcile with its own totals, so I used the hours figures only.
