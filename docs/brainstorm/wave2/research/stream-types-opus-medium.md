# What gets watched on Twitch, and what small streamers stream (research, 2026-10-26)

## Summary

- **Just Chatting is #1 by a wide margin.** It had about 303.6K average viewers, or 14.2% of Twitch viewing, in the last 7 days of October 2026 [S1]. In Q1 2026 it had 668.2M of Twitch's 4.55B hours watched, about 14.7% [S3].
- **The rest of the top 15 is mostly big competitive and sandbox games.** These include League of Legends, Minecraft, GTA V, Counter-Strike, the new Call of Duty, VALORANT, Fortnite, Dota 2 and WoW. IRL (#11) is the only other non-game category in the top 15 [S1].
- **Non-gaming viewing is growing.** It was 22% of Twitch viewing in 2025, up from 20% in 2024. Just Chatting grew +25% and IRL +19% (Stream Hatchet) [S5]. Twitch's own recap says IRL watch time "nearly tripled" [S6]. Sports fell 11% across Twitch and Kick [S5].
- **Mid-size non-gaming categories:** Sports (#29), ASMR (#34), Art (#40), DJs (#41), Music (#52), Talk Shows (~#66), Pools (~#122), Software & Game Dev (~#139), Food & Drink (~#170), Makers & Crafting (~#206), Science & Tech (~#229) [S1, S2].
- **Growing formats:** horror (287M hours in Jan to Aug 2026, +6% YoY) and cozy "creatures and companions" games (142M hours, a new trend) [S7]. Big one-off events and creator-run shows (La Velada, GP Explorer) set the peaks [S5, S6].
- **Small streamers:** I found no primary dataset that breaks down categories for channels under 50 viewers. Viewers-per-channel ratios suggest Fortnite (about 11), Art (about 17), Music (about 14), Software Dev (about 11) and Makers (about 9) are crowded relative to their audience. Ratios are much higher in IRL (about 75), Just Chatting (about 66) and ASMR (about 68), but those averages are dominated by big channels [S2, S8].
- **The platform is flat to shrinking, and Kick is growing.** Twitch hours fell 13.7% YoY in Q1 2026, while Kick grew 65.4% YoY [S3]. Twitch averages about 2.18M viewers across about 91K live channels, roughly 24 viewers per live channel [S4, S8].

## 4. Small streamers (under about 50 viewers)

- **Overall distribution.** Twitch averages about 24 viewers per live channel (2,225,996 viewers ÷ 91,096 channels, 7 days to 15 Aug 2026) [S8]. Viewing is very top-heavy. A widely repeated claim says about 95% of channels average 0 to 5 viewers [S12], but it comes from a blog estimate, not Twitch data, so I could not verify it.
- **Where small streamers go.** I found no category breakdown for channels under 50 viewers. As a proxy, Fortnite has the most live channels of any category (5.5K in Aug, 4.3K in Oct) and the most hours *streamed* on Twitch in Q1 2026 (10.6M h) [S3, S8]. Across all platforms, Just Chatting has the most hours streamed (13.3M) [S3]. *Inference:* small channels cluster in Fortnite, Just Chatting, VALORANT, Apex, Dead by Daylight, Minecraft and GTA RP.
- **Saturation (viewers per live channel, Aug 2026)** [S8]:

  A high ratio does not mean a small streamer can easily get viewers there. The averages are dominated by big channels.
- **Medians are a better guide.** The highest median viewers are in tiny niches, such as Rise Online (63.6 median, 14 channels), Heroes of Might & Magic III (19.4, 25 channels), Poker (13.1, 67 channels) and DJs (10.6, 212 channels) [S8].
- **Advice for small streamers.** Pick categories with roughly 500 to 5,000 viewers and fewer than 200 streamers. Use the median viewer count, not the average, and use the Time-to-Stream analytics tool [S8, S13]. This is blog heuristics, not measured outcomes.

## 5. Trends 2025–2026

- **Twitch:**
  - 19.2B hours in 2025, with market share down 8.3 points [S5].
  - Q4 2025 had 4.4B hours, the lowest since 2020. Unique channels were 8.4M in Q4 2025 (lowest since Q1 2020) [S5] and 8.74M in Q1 2026 (−5.5% YoY) [S3].
  - Q1 2026 had 4.55B hours (−13.7% YoY), though up 3.9% on the previous quarter [S3].
- **Kick:**
  - 4.5B hours in 2025 (+131%) [S5].
  - 1.27B hours in Q1 2026 (+65% YoY), with 303.8M of them in Just Chatting [S3].
- **Non-gaming:** rising from 20% to 22% of Twitch viewing [S5].
- **Vertical video and discovery:**
  - The clips discovery feed was announced in 2023 [S11].
  - A 2026 vertical-livestream test is reported only by a single secondary source [S14], so it is unverified.
- **Twitch's own 2025 recap headline:** "nearly 900 million hours" [S6]. This conflicts with Stream Hatchet's 19.2B and probably measures something narrower, so treat it with caution.
