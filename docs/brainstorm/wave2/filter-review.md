# Review of wave2/filter.md (step 3, hard filter)

Reviewer: independent audit. I re-read the revised inventory.md (38 clusters), filter.md, brief.md section 3, the five sources and the video clause in rules.md (Submission Requirements). I did not read synthesis.md or process.md, and I edited nothing except this file.

## Verdict

The filter is usable and the bookkeeping is right (23 Pass + 4 Test (low) + 7 Test (high) + 4 Fail = 38, and 34 clusters go on). Most marks match the sources. The weak points are consistency, not bad facts. (1) Dependencies on a second live channel, on local speech-to-text, and on "standard" Helix calls are classed differently across clusters that carry the same risk. (2) The "pass if one variant passes" rule is applied to some clusters and ignored for others. (3) Cluster-level outcomes hide that several full cards are themselves Test (high) or Fail. (4) The day-1 list misses a few dependencies. All four Fails are L3-only clusters, and three of them rest on the F6 judgement that is applied loosely elsewhere. I recommend fixing findings 1–5 before step 4. Judgement calls are marked **[J]**.
