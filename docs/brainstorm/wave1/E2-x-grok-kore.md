You're connected to X as @neolight11. Here's what I can do:

- **Your account**: your profile, home timeline, your posts, and mentions
- **Posts**: open any post from a link, and see who liked, reposted, or quoted it
- **Users**: look up any account by handle, search for users, and read their posts
- **Search**: search posts across X and count post volume on a topic
- **News & trends**: search X news stories and get trends by location
- **Spaces & communities**: search live or scheduled Spaces, see who's hosting and what was shared, and look up communities
- **Lists**: read any list's posts and members, and create, edit, follow, pin, and manage your own lists
- **Bookmarks**: list, add, and remove bookmarks, and organize them into folders
- **Chat**: read and reply to X Chat, see what's unread, and go through message requests (needs your Chat PIN by secret-request, never pasted here)
- **Calls**: list the X calls on your calendar, schedule or change one, and RSVP
- **Livestreams**: your stream sources and broadcasts, schedule a broadcast, summarize a past stream's chat, and manage moderators and mutes

Posting and going live right now aren't supported.

## 1. Method

Searched X only, 1 Feb 2024–25 Oct 2026 (`search_posts_all`, relevancy, `-is:retweet`). About **170 rows** across 16 queries; about **40** were on-topic opinions. The rest were patch roundups, promos, game login events, or spam. Counts below are slices, not population estimates. Follower counts are as of this lookup, not the day of the post.

Korean: `치지직 (기능 OR 장점) (트위치)`, `트위치에 없는` / `트위치에는 없는` (0 hits), `치지직 (확대 OR 줌)`, `치지직 (예측 OR 치트키 OR 타임머신 OR 오디오 모드)`, `(애청자 OR 출석체크 OR 팬 레벨) (숲 OR 치지직)`, `(캐치 OR 방명록 OR 방송국) (숲 OR 아프리카)`, `(라디오 모드 OR 오디오 모드) 치지직`, `(아프리카 OR 숲) 다시보기 (바로 OR 빨라 OR 채팅)`, `숲 캐치` / `아프리카 캐치`, `치지직 (저지연 OR 이모티콘 모드 OR 드롭스 OR 팔로우 알림)`, `트위치 그립` / `트위치가 그리워`, `(타임머신 OR 치트키) (치지직 OR 숲)`.

English: `(chzzk OR SOOP) (twitch) (feature OR chat OR emote OR better)`, `(chzzk OR afreeca OR soop) (zoom OR rewind OR "chat replay")`.

`출석 체크` and `팬 레벨` did not return on-topic posts about a free viewer tool. Almost every hit was a game login event, a promo, or someone using "애청자" as a follower count.

What even is Log Power. It makes me not want to collect them."
