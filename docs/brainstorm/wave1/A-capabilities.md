## Hypotheses check

- "Affiliate/Partner-only features are unavailable": **partly wrong, partly right.** The EventSub type list has no Affiliate statement. Channel Points opened to all in May 2026 [V]. Commercials, charity, stream delay and sub lookups remain gated [V]. Polls/predictions/hype train remain unresolved.
- "Judges judge from video": not a capability question, no evidence collected here.
- "Many entrants near examples": no evidence collected. Local overview shows 473 participants at the time of capture, nothing about what they build.

## Open questions

2. Does Create Custom Reward (Channel Points) still 403 on a non-Affiliate that completed the new onboarding? Docs are stale or still true. Test.
3. Does the new account have chat restrictions (phone verification, account age) for sending messages via API? IRC page mentions phone verification can be required [V]; scope unclear.
4. Real chat-to-EventSub and glass-to-glass stream latency: measure once with two accounts and Video Stats.
5. Is forwarding chat text to an LLM provider allowed under the Developer Agreement's no-sharing/24 h rule? Contract language ambiguous; consider only sending minimal text, or ask in TwitchDev Discord.
6. Help-center pages (Affiliate, Hype Train, fake engagement, game-footage rules) could not be read; confirm in the Creator Dashboard (Analytics, Achievements).
7. Rate-limit budget for Helix is not published as a number (only the token-bucket model and an example of 800) [V]. Per-endpoint limits found: raids 10/10 min, shoutout 1/2 min, announcements 1/2 s.
