## Verification of lead claims
**a. Bilibili fan medal and TikTok fan club: per-channel loyalty level beside the name, rising with engagement.**
- Bilibili: **Confirmed with a big caveat.** The level appears with each chat message [V]. But free engagement now yields at most 30 intimacy/day by a community doc (2026-05-14 rules) and 98% cuts in 2025 per a critic; paid actions dominate. Official Bilibili pages were behind a captcha, so I read only third-party sources. Level scale was merged to 1-120 on 2025-09-08 [V, community doc].
- TikTok: **Partly confirmed.** Levels, missions (watch, comment, gift) and badges beside names at higher levels are [V]. Joining needs a gift; free-ness unverified. Region-limited, Asia-first.

**b. TikTok LIVE and YouTube Live have native Q&A.**
- YouTube: **Confirmed [V].** Start Q&A from chat, pick a question, it pins to top of chat for viewers.
- TikTok: **Not verified.** Newsroom (2021-03-04) says LIVE creators can see questions in a separate Q&A panel [V], but not how a question is picked or highlighted on screen. Support pages returned empty.

**c. YouTube Live polls free for all creators.** **Supported, 2021 evidence.** Mactrast (July 2021) says polls and subscriber-only chat became "available to all streamers", no thresholds (thresholds applied to clips and trailers) [V]. Current help page lists no requirements but is also silent about eligibility [V]. Live streaming itself needs channel verification and no recent live restrictions [S].

**d. Twitch equivalent.** Partial.
- Watch Streaks: consecutive-stream attendance, bonus Channel Points (300-450 for 2-5+ streams), each stream at least 10 minutes and 30 minutes apart [S, Twitch help snippet; page failed to load]. Rolled out to all channels at TwitchCon 2025 [S]. Shared via chat notification; exposed in IRC tags June 2026 [S]. Streamer.bot has a WatchStreak event [V].
- Sub badges: paid, tenure-based.
- **Missing:** a visible, cumulative, non-paid, per-channel level that rises from several action types (watch, chat, like) and displays beside the name every message.

## Gaps and null results
- SOOP: no free-viewer mechanism found. Searches returned esports supporter badges (streamer-side) and a streamer emblem tier system, 2026-09-29 [S, mtn.co.kr] (success unverified).
- Korean migrants' praise or missed features: only launch-era articles (2024) saying CHZZK copied Twitch's UI and added video donation and sub-tenure merge; no first-hand praise found.
- Bilibili free interactive features beyond medals (lucky draw 天选时刻, likes): searches returned nothing usable. Danmaku live features beyond C: none found.
- Inaccessible: Bilibili official posts (captcha), TikTok support pages (empty), Twitch viewer help page (CSS error), x.com tweet (402), arXiv PDFs (binary).
- Platform-level user love evidence for any single free mechanism is thin. The most solid demand signals are the two automation tools (10k and 1.4k stars), which show both interest and metric gaming.
