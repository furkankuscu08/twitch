## Important memories

- **Deadline:** November 2, 2026 at 14:00 Pacific = **November 3 at 01:00 in Istanbul** (22:00 UTC on November 2). The FAQ's 21:00 UTC conversion is one hour early. Based on Rules §1 and timezone conversion accounting for daylight saving time.
- **Build context:** The project must be new work within the submission period, use Codex, and connect to Twitch. Keep the primary build thread's session ID for submission. See Rules §4 and the FAQ's **Technical and Codex** section.
- **Video ambiguity:** Rules §4 requires a publicly visible YouTube demo; the FAQ permits unlisted videos. Public visibility satisfies both wordings. No visibility decision or organizer clarification is recorded.
- **Video content:** No third-party trademarks, copyrighted music, or other copyrighted material (including games and other streamers) in the video without permission. Plan demos around our own content (IRL desk, drawing, talking) or games whose terms allow it. Rules §4 (line 79), FAQ.
- **Timing risk:** The FAQ warns Extension review can take 5–14 days and judges cannot install unreviewed Extensions. Remember this when choosing the project approach.
